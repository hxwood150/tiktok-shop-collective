What This Should Do for a Reader

When someone views my collection in December, I want them to think critically about their buying and viewing habits. Advertisements and influencer culture surrounds us, so there must be some sort of pattern embedded in our feeds that may or may not implicitly impact us when we view content online. The viewer may come to realize that they are easily influenced. Sometimes, we scroll on past ads without even stopping to watch, but when all the ads are on the screen as a collective, we may stop to realize something bigger.

Guardrails: all captures come from public TikTok accounts and public posts. No private information, private communications, or friends-only content is included. Inappropriate or offensive products are excluded on sight. This project studies algorithmic persuasion, not the people behind it.
